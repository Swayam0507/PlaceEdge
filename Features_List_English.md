# Key Features of the Smart Placement Preparation Platform

Here is the complete list of features to add to your PPT. You can divide these into 2-3 slides depending on your presentation style.

---

### 1. AI-Powered Resume ATS Checker
* Analyzes uploaded resumes (PDF/DOCX) using Google Gemini AI.
* Calculates an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) score and provides actionable suggestions to improve the resume.

### 2. Dynamic AI Mock Exams
* Instantly generates customized multiple-choice questions (MCQs) for any technical or aptitude category.
* Users can select difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard) to test their knowledge.

### 3. Machine Learning Placement Predictor
* Uses advanced ML algorithms (Python/Django) to predict a student's probability of getting placed based on their academic profile and test scores.

### 4. Smart Job Recommendations
* Analyzes a student's skills and strengths to recommend the most suitable job roles and career paths.

### 5. Real-Time Job Scraper
* Automatically fetches and displays the latest job openings and internships from various job boards across the internet.

### 6. Interactive AI Chatbot
* A 24/7 virtual assistant ready to answer student queries, explain complex concepts, and provide interview tips.

### 7. Performance Analytics & Reports
* Tracks all past exam attempts and visualizes the student's progress using interactive charts and graphs.
* Allows users to download their detailed performance reports in PDF format.

### 8. Community Discussion Forum
* A collaborative space where students can ask questions, share interview experiences, and discuss technical topics with peers.

### 9. Secure User Authentication
* Highly secure Login and Registration system using JWT (JSON Web Tokens) and encrypted passwords.
* Includes email verification and password recovery features.

### 10. Admin Management Dashboard
* A dedicated panel for administrators to manage users, monitor platform usage, and oversee the content effortlessly.
